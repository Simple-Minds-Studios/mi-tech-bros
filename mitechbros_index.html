04 / FAQ

The practical stuff.

Where are you based?

Western Michigan is home base. Travel outside our normal area may be possible when schedules and the job make sense.

Do you have regular storefront hours?

No. MITechBros. works by appointment around our existing schedules. That flexibility is part of the model, not an accident.

Will I always get both of you?

No. Some jobs need one person. Some benefit from both. We match the work to availability and experience rather than billing extra bodies for no reason.

Do I have to know what is wrong?

Absolutely not. “It keeps doing this weird thing” is often enough to start. Diagnosis is part of the work.

Do you repair every kind of hardware?

No. We troubleshoot broadly, but we will not pretend to do specialized board-level repair or advanced data recovery if the job belongs with a specialist. We can help identify the next step.

Do you teach, or just fix things?

Both. We like leaving people more confident with their technology, so explanation and education are part of the service whenever you want them.
